# Research note: time-tracking tools (checked 7 Nov 2026)

Source: each vendor's own pricing, help or affiliate page unless noted. USD.

## Toggl Track (toggl.com/track/pricing)
- Free: $0. "Free for a limited number of users" (FAQ: up to 3 users for teams). Timers, calendar integrations, tasks, personal-level reports.
- Starter: $9 and $12 per user/month shown (annual/monthly toggle, "save up to 33% a year"; page doesn't label which is which, lower = annual). Adds billable rates.
- Premium: $16 and $24 per user/month shown. Enterprise: contact.
- 30-day trial on Starter and Premium.
- Invoices (support.toggl.com, toggl.com/features/time-tracking-invoicing): create a PDF invoice from Summary/Detailed reports; cannot send from the app, download PDF only; send to QuickBooks Online is a Starter feature. Billable amounts need billable rates (paid). Free-plan PDFs carry the Toggl logo (community.toggl.com).
- Affiliate (toggl.com/track/affiliates): 30% of the FIRST payment of each new paid Toggl Track organization. No need to be a customer. Runs on PartnerStack.

## Clockify (clockify.me/pricing)
- Free: $0, up to 5 users, unlimited projects and tracking. Reports cover a 1-month data range.
- Basic $3.99 annual / $4.99 monthly per seat: billable rates.
- Standard $5.49 annual / $6.99 monthly: invoicing, QuickBooks.
- Pro $7.99 / $9.99. Enterprise $11.99 / $14.99. Bundle $12.99 / $15.99.
- Every active or invited user = one paid seat.
- Free trial length not stated on pricing page.
- Affiliate (cake.com/affiliate): 30% of referrals' purchases; payouts stop after one year or when the user cancels. Covers Clockify, Pumble, Plaky. Payout PayPal/wire, $100 min (third-party: uppromote).

## Harvest (getharvest.com/pricing)
- Free: $0, 1 seat, 2 projects; time tracking, invoicing, expense tracking, Mac/iOS apps. Page: "usage of some features may require the payment of fees" (details in Help Center, not listed).
- Teams: $9/seat/month annual ($108/yr), $11 monthly. Enterprise: $14 annual ($168/yr), $17.50 monthly. Enterprise Plus: custom.
- Teams/Enterprise: base rate + "additional invoices, projects, clients, and tasks are billed based on use". Allowances and per-unit fees NOT published on the page.
- Stripe, QuickBooks Online, Xero integrations on Teams and Enterprise, not Free.
- 30-day trial on paid plans, no card.
- Affiliate program: none found.
- Third-party (productive.io, timecamp, both competitors): Bending Spoons bought Harvest in 2025; plan names changed from Pro/Premium to Teams/Enterprise with usage fees. Not used in the guide.
