# Research note: invoicing tools (checked 7 Nov 2026)

Source: each vendor's own pricing or help page unless noted. USD.

## FreshBooks (freshbooks.com/pricing)
- Lite $23/mo: 5 billable clients. Plus $43/mo: 50 clients, proposals, retainers, double-entry reports. Premium $70/mo: unlimited clients. Select: custom.
- Intro promo shown: 80% off for 4 months. Yearly billing: extra 10% off.
- 30-day free trial, no credit card.
- Add-ons: team members $11/mo each; Advanced Payments $20/mo.
- Time tracking and recurring invoices on all plans.
- Payments (support.freshbooks.com): cards 2.9% + $0.30; Amex 3.5% + $0.30; bank transfer (ACH, US) 1%. International card +1.5% for US accounts; currency conversion +2%. FreshBooks Payments accounts are US or Canada.

## HoneyBook (honeybook.com/pricing)
- Starter $29/mo billed yearly (about $36 monthly). Essentials $49 yearly (about $59 monthly). Premium $109 yearly (about $129 monthly). Monthly figures appear as struck-through prices; confirm at checkout.
- Starter: unlimited clients and projects, invoices and payments, proposals and contracts, client portal, basic reports.
- Essentials adds: scheduler, automations, QuickBooks Online, up to 2 team members, removes "Powered by HoneyBook".
- Free trial, no card. 60-day money-back guarantee.
- Card fees: page shows both "from 2.7% + 10¢" and "from 2.9% + 25¢" (inconsistent). Bank transfer 1.5%.
- Note: help.honeybook.com still lists older 2024 prices ($19/$39/$79). Pricing page is newer.
- Available only to businesses in the US, Canada, UK and Australia (HoneyBook press release, 28 May 2026).

## Wave (waveapps.com/pricing)
- Starter $0: unlimited invoices, estimates, bills.
- Pro $19/mo or $190/yr: automatic late-payment reminders, deposits via estimates, your logo and colors, removes Wave branding, attachments.
- Cards: 2.9% + $0.60 (Amex 3.4% + $0.60). Pro: no $0.60 on the first 10 card payments each month.
- Online payments need approval. Pricing in USD/CAD; built for US and Canadian businesses.

## Zoho Invoice (zoho.com/invoice/pricing)
- Free forever. Limits: 2 users, 3 projects, 500 invoices a year, 2 organizations. "Powered by Zoho Invoice" branding.
- Includes recurring invoices, automated payment reminders, client portal, time tracking, online payments via connected gateways.
- Zoho's page doesn't state its own payment fee; third-party review (saascrmreview, Oct 2026) says Zoho adds none. Gateway (Stripe, PayPal etc.) charges its normal rate.
