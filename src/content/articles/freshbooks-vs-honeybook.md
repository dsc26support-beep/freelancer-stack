Quick answer

- **Pick HoneyBook** if every job starts with a proposal, a contract and a deposit (designers, photographers, coaches, event pros) and you're based in the US, Canada, UK or Australia.
- **Pick FreshBooks** if you mainly bill for time or monthly retainers and want your bookkeeping done in the same place, or if you live outside those four countries.

## The real question: bookkeeper or booking system?

Both tools send invoices and take card payments. That's where the overlap ends.

**FreshBooks** starts from the money. It tracks your time, sends invoices, records expenses and (on Plus and up) produces proper accounting reports for tax time.

**HoneyBook** starts from the client. It takes an enquiry, sends a proposal and contract, books a call, then invoices. It's built to win and manage the job, not to do your books.

So ask yourself: *what eats more of my week, chasing clients through the booking process, or sorting out money afterwards?* That answer usually picks the tool for you.

<div class="catch">

The catch most people miss: FreshBooks' 5-client limit

FreshBooks Lite looks cheapest, but it only lets you bill **5 clients**. The day you land client number six, you're moving to Plus at $43 a month. If you expect more than five clients a year, compare HoneyBook against FreshBooks *Plus*, not Lite, and HoneyBook comes out about $168 a year cheaper.

</div>

## Fees per invoice

On a **$1,000 invoice paid by card**, you'd pay about **$29.30** with FreshBooks and roughly **$27 to $29** with HoneyBook. That's close enough not to matter.

Bank transfers are where they differ. On the same $1,000 paid by bank transfer, FreshBooks takes **$10** (1%, US only) and HoneyBook takes **$15** (1.5%). If your clients pay large invoices by bank, FreshBooks keeps more in your pocket.

## Who should skip each one

**Skip FreshBooks if** your work is project-based and you need contracts signed before you start. You'd end up adding a separate contract tool and paying twice.

**Skip HoneyBook if** you're outside the US, Canada, UK or Australia, because you can't sign up. Also skip it if you mostly bill hours, or want your accounting handled in the same tool.
