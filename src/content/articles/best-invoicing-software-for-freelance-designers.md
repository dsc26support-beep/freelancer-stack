Quick answer

- **Best for most project-based designers: HoneyBook.** Proposal, contract and deposit in one go. US, Canada, UK and Australia only.
- **Best for retainers and hourly work: FreshBooks.** Time tracking, recurring invoices and proper books.
- **Best free option in most countries: Zoho Invoice.** Free forever, with automatic payment reminders.
- **Best free option in the US or Canada: Wave.** Unlimited free invoices, but no automatic reminders unless you pay.

</div>

## What designers really need from an invoice tool

Most "best invoicing software" lists are written for shops and accountants. Designers get paid differently. Before you compare tools, check which of these jobs you actually have:

1. **Deposits.** You want 30–50% before you open Figma.
2. **Scope protection.** A signed contract that says "two rounds of revisions" before the work starts.
3. **Retainers.** The same invoice every month to the same client, sent automatically.
4. **Overseas clients.** A client in another currency, paying by card.
5. **Chasing.** Automatic reminders so you don't have to write "just following up…" again.

The right tool is the one that covers *your* jobs on its cheapest plan.

## Cost per year, and per invoice

Say you send **4 invoices a month at $750 each, paid by card**:

| | Subscription per year | Card fees per year | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zoho Invoice | $0 | ~$1,058.40 (at a typical 2.9% + 30¢ gateway rate) | **~$1,058** |
| Wave (free) | $0 | $1,072.80 | **$1,072.80** |
| FreshBooks Lite | $276 | $1,058.40 | **$1,334.40** |
| HoneyBook Starter | $348 | ~$977–1,056 | **~$1,325–1,404** |

Two things jump out. First, **card fees cost far more than any subscription**, so ask clients to pay by bank transfer when you can. Second, the paid tools add **$23 to $29 a month** on top. The question is whether a signed contract and automatic chasing are worth that to you. For most designers who've been burned by scope creep once, they are.

<div class="catch">

The catch: overseas clients cost extra

If you bill clients in other countries, check the small print. FreshBooks adds **1.5%** for international cards (US accounts) and **2%** if the invoice currency differs from your account's. Wave's payments are built for US and Canadian businesses. If most of your clients are abroad, compare these extras before the headline price.

</div>

## Who should skip each one

- **HoneyBook:** skip it if you live outside the US, Canada, UK or Australia (you can't sign up), or if you mostly bill hours.
- **FreshBooks:** skip it if you need signed contracts before starting work. You'd pay for a second tool.
- **Zoho Invoice:** skip it if you want your own branding only. Free invoices carry "Powered by Zoho Invoice".
- **Wave:** skip it if you're outside the US or Canada, or if you need automatic reminders without paying for Pro.
