If you invoice a handful of clients a month, you do not need a full accounting suite. You need to send a clear invoice, get paid on time, and not lose track of who owes what. This guide helps you match a tool to how you actually work.

## What designers should look for

- Recurring invoices for retainer clients
- Deposits or milestone payments for project work
- Multiple currencies, if you have overseas clients
- Time tracking, if you bill hourly
- Fees: payment processing fees often matter more than the subscription price
