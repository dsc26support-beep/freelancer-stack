Quick answer

- **Pick Zoho Invoice** if you want the most features free: automatic payment reminders, recurring invoices and a client portal, in most countries.
- **Pick Wave** if you're in the US or Canada and want unlimited invoices plus free basic bookkeeping, and you don't mind chasing late payers yourself.
- **Start paying** only when you need contracts, more than 500 invoices a year, or your own branding.

</div>

## "Free" usually isn't. These two are.

Search "free invoicing software" and most results are **free trials** that end in 14 or 30 days. Zoho Invoice and Wave are different: their free plans don't expire. They make money in other ways. Wave earns on payment fees and its paid Pro plan. Zoho points bigger businesses to its paid apps, such as Zoho Billing.

## The hidden cost: Wave's 60¢

Wave's card fee is 2.9% **plus 60 cents** on every payment. That fixed 60¢ hurts small invoices most:

| Invoice amount | Wave fee | As a share of the invoice |
|---|---|---|
| $50 | $2.05 | 4.1% |
| $200 | $6.40 | 3.2% |
| $1,000 | $29.60 | 3.0% |

If you send lots of small invoices, that's real money. A common card rate of 2.9% + 30¢, as Stripe and PayPal often charge, would save you 30¢ on each one. Check your gateway's current rate for your country. Wave Pro drops the 60¢ on your first 10 card payments each month.

<div class="catch">

The catch with Wave's free plan: you do the chasing

Automatic late-payment reminders are a **Wave Pro** feature. On the free plan you have to notice a late invoice and email the client yourself. If chasing money is the part you hate, Zoho Invoice does it for free.

</div>

## When free stops being enough

Free tools are the right call when you're starting out. Upgrade when one of these happens:

- **You need a contract signed before you start.** Neither free tool does contracts. Look at [HoneyBook](/articles/freshbooks-vs-honeybook/).
- **You bill more than 500 invoices a year** (Zoho's cap). That's about 40 a month.
- **Branding matters.** Bigger clients notice "Powered by…" on your invoices.
- **Tax time is painful.** FreshBooks' Plus plan gives proper accounting reports. See [FreshBooks vs HoneyBook](/articles/freshbooks-vs-honeybook/).

## Who should skip each one

- **Skip Zoho Invoice** if you also want free bookkeeping. It handles invoices and expenses, not full accounts.
- **Skip Wave** if you're outside the US or Canada, or if you send many small invoices by card.
