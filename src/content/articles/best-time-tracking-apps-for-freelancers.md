Quick answer

- **Pick Toggl Track (free)** if you want to start tracking today and turn your hours into a PDF invoice without paying anything.
- **Pick Clockify Standard** if you want billable rates and invoices for the lowest price: $5.49 a month, billed yearly.
- **Pick Harvest (free)** only if you have two clients or fewer. Its paid plans now add usage fees it doesn't publish.
- **Already pay for FreshBooks or Zoho Invoice?** Both include time tracking. Don't buy another app.

</div>

## Why bother tracking time at all?

Because the hours you don't log are hours you don't bill. If you charge $40 an hour and forget to log just 15 minutes a day, that's **60 hours, or $2,400, a year** you worked for free.

Even if you charge per project, tracking tells you your real hourly rate. That's how you spot the client who quietly pays you half what the others do.

## What a year costs if you bill from tracked time

For a solo freelancer who wants hourly rates applied automatically and an invoice at the end of the month:

| App and plan | Paid yearly | Paid monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Clockify Standard | **$65.88** | $83.88 |
| Toggl Track Starter | **$108** | $144 |
| Harvest Teams | **$108 + usage fees** | $132 + usage fees |
| FreshBooks or Zoho Invoice you already use | **$0 extra** | $0 extra |

Clockify costs about $42 a year less than Toggl Track for the same basic job. Paying yearly instead of monthly saves another $18 to $36.

<div class="catch">

The catch: Harvest's new usage fees

Harvest's pricing page says its paid plans have a base price, and that extra invoices, projects, clients and tasks are then billed by use. It doesn't say how much is included or what each extra costs. Until it does, you can't know your real bill in advance. Start the free trial and check the billing page before you commit.

</div>

## Who should skip each one

- **Skip Toggl Track** if you want to email invoices from the app. It only creates a PDF to download, or sends the invoice to QuickBooks on paid plans.
- **Skip Clockify's free plan** if you need reports covering more than a month at a time. Also note that invoicing needs the Standard plan; Basic only adds billable rates.
- **Skip Harvest** if you have more than two clients and don't want surprise usage charges. The free plan stops at 2 projects.
